  explicit zero cycle (cavities cannot change that arithmetic) and is `missing_cavities` with a
  nonzero cycle; zero cavities stay invalid; a zero quantity keeps its `invalid_quantity` issue and
  expands to a zero line total; an empty part list keeps its zero totals with `no_parts`.

  missing-versus-explicit-zero rules, the checked-decimal boundary, the excluded categories and the
