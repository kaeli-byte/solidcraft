The six tooling domain tests use real `Store::calculate_baseline` snapshots (a delivered product,
a registered exact analysis, quoted forecasts and explicit purchased sourcing where the cutoff
matters), never a hand-inserted baseline row:
- `tooling_settings_are_append_only_idempotent_and_validated_against_the_project`: a settings write
  appends one project record version (v3 -> v4), an identical retry replays without a new version, a
  second write appends v5 while v4 stays readable, a stale `expected_version` and a changed payload
  under a used request id are conflicts, and an unknown part/product owner or a lower-case currency
  is refused as invalid.
- `a_recovery_evaluation_counts_investment_once_and_never_recovers_it_twice`: two 1 × 10,000 CNY
  entries give a project investment of 20,000 once and 10,000 per tool; manual 5,000 gives a
  contribution of 2 per owner unit, an allocation share of 1, an allocated investment of 10,000 and
  a product contribution of 2; the two tools sum into a product amortized contribution of 4 with a
  projected recovery of 4,000 and -16,000 under/over. The same baseline under the separate
  treatment keeps the 20,000 investment as a 20,000 separate charge with every amortized field
  `null`.
- `an_assembly_quantity_and_the_stored_forecasts_drive_the_contribution_and_the_under_over`: an
  assembly used twice with four forecast years of 1,000 (4,000 products, 8,000 owner units) gives a
  contribution of 2, a product contribution of 4, an annual recovery of 4,000, a projected 16,000
  and +6,000 under/over; manual 10,000 gives -2,000; the lifetime basis gives a recovery quantity of
  8,000, a contribution of 1.25, a projected 10,000 and 0 under/over.
- `a_manual_recovery_quantity_below_the_lifetime_demand_under_recovers_the_investment`: lifetime
  4,000 with manual 5,000 gives 2 per unit, a projected 8,000, -2,000 under/over and 2,000 recovered
  in each of four years.
- `a_tool_only_under_a_purchased_branch_has_zero_combined_demand_and_an_incomplete_result`: the
  baseline product line holds only the purchased parent; a tool owned by the suppressed descendant
  reports a 10,000 investment with an unavailable recovery quantity/contribution/projection and the
  incomplete `zero_combined_demand` issue.
- `the_recovery_evaluation_is_read_only_and_refuses_unknown_inputs`: an evaluation appends no
  project version and no snapshot and repeats deterministically; after a later settings write moves
  the current project version to a different default treatment (separate) with no entries, the
  earlier baseline still answers from the tooling settings of the project version it recorded
  (amortized, the 10,000 entry), proving the snapshot's own version is read; it returns
  `Invalid`/`NotFound` for a nil or unknown baseline.
