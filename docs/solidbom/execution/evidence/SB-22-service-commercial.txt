Seeded fixture (all through the real routes)
--------------------------------------------

The published reference data and the app cost configurations are the deterministic test fixtures the
service publishes itself (material PA12 density 2.7 g/cm3 and 20 CNY/kg, one production and one setup
cost centre at 120 CNY/h); no company default is invented. The script seeds:

1. Project DEM (`00000000-0000-0000-0000-000022d00001`, default schedule 2027-2027, currency CNY).
   Products A and B each override the schedule to 2027-2028 and deliver the same two shared part
   versions: made part M-1/A (net consumption 100 cm3 of the seeded material, material allowance 2%,
   one part routing with a 30 s per-piece cycle at the seeded production rate and a dedicated 0.5 h
   setup at the seeded setup rate) and purchased part P-1/A (confirmed catalog association, then a
   project purchase-tier table in USD with saved rate 7.2). Forecasts: A 100/200, B 200/100 for
   2027/2028. Project volume policy: maximum.
2. Project COMMERCIAL (`00000000-0000-0000-0000-000022c00001`, default schedule 2027-2029, currency
   CNY). One quoted product inherits the schedule, delivers purchased part P-80/A (confirmed catalog
   single price 80 CNY), has forecasts 100/200/300, one product-root setup operation of 0.5 h at the
   seeded setup rate and no production cycle cost, project volume policy average, commercial setting
   EBIT target 0.2 and annual productivity 0.03, and one tool entry: 1 x 10,000 CNY tool owned by
   part version P-80/A with project default treatment amortized. Five named scenarios are saved
   against the same immutable baseline.
3. Project MISSING (`00000000-0000-0000-0000-000022e00001`, default schedule 2027-2028). One quoted
   product inherits the schedule, delivers purchased part P-10/A (catalog price 10 CNY), and has a
   forecast for 2027 only. Volume policy maximum. One scenario payload deliberately also carries
   `volume_policy` and `ebit_target_rate` fields that the contract does not define.
4. Project PRICING (`00000000-0000-0000-0000-000022f00001`, schedule 2027-2027). One quoted product
   with forecast 100 delivers purchased part P-UNCOVERED/A, which has a project tier whose smallest
   minimum quantity (400) exceeds the costing volume (100) and has no confirmed catalog price.
5. Project ADVANCED (`00000000-0000-0000-0000-000023000001`, schedule 2027-2027). Product X (forecast
   100) contains a purchased assembly PA-1/A whose manifest children C-1/A and D-1/A are inside the
   purchased branch, plus its own root occurrence of W-1/A. Product Y (forecast 50) directly contains
   C-1/A, W-1/A and two occurrences of S-1/A. The latest published app configuration declares a
   direct-material view category, a fixed 5-unit overhead category and a 10% direct-cost percentage
   category. Six tool entries are written so that product-root, purchased-assembly, shared,
   repeated-occurrence and suppressed-branch tooling are all evaluated.

The script registers one exact analysis manifest per product revision and writes sourcing, routings,
catalog confirmations, costing settings, tooling settings, commercial settings and saved scenarios
through the versioned routes. The raw status and body of the writes are in the captures; the
observations below are the reads that follow them.

What the capture shows (raw bodies are in the next section)
-----------------------------------------------------------

1. **A11 demand order.** The made part M-1/A and the purchased part P-1/A are each demanded by both
   quoted products. The baseline returns annual demand 300 for 2027, 300 for 2028, a peak demand of
   300 and lifetime demand 600. The 300 comes from adding the two products' forecasts in the same
   calendar year (100 + 200 and 200 + 100) before applying the project's maximum volume policy;
   the peak is 300, not the 400 sum of the individual product peaks. Costing volume is 300.
2. **A12 missing forecast.** The MISSING baseline is `incomplete` with the named issue
   `missing_forecast`. Its only part's demand is [{2027, 100}, {2028, null}]: the absent 2028 forecast
   is a missing value, never a zero. Lifetime demand and costing volume stay `null`. A saved scenario
   whose content payload carries a `volume_policy` of `average` and an `ebit_target_rate` of `0.5` is
   stored without those fields (the scenario contract has no such fields), and the comparison still
   reports the project's volume policy `maximum` and its (absent) project EBIT target `null`. A
   scenario cannot change the project volume policy.
3. **A13 setup.** The made part M-1/A routing is shared by both quoted products and carries a
   dedicated 0.5 h setup at the seeded 120/h setup rate. The setup charge is 60, the setup total is
   60 (counted once per project quotation, not 120 for the two products, and never amortized over the
   demand of 300), and the part's per-piece unit cost is 6.508 (material 5.508 + production 1) with
   the 60 outside it. The product unit total is 64.108 (6.508 made + 57.6 purchased) and does not
   contain the setup charge.
4. **A14 productivity.** The COMMERCIAL baseline has recurring unit cost C = 80. With project EBIT
   target 0.2 and annual productivity 0.03, the suggested base price and the manual base price
   series are 100, 97 and 94.09 for 2027, 2028 and 2029 (the product's own first production year is
   2027), while `recurring_unit_cost` stays 80 in every year: manufacturing cost does not fall with
   the selling price.
5. **A15 tooling investment and alternatives.** One tool entry of 1 x 10,000 CNY is the whole
   project investment, counted once. Evaluating the amortized view with a manual recovery quantity of
   5,000 returns investment 10,000, recovery quantity 5,000, contribution 2 per owner unit, product
   amortized contribution 2 (the owner occurs once per finished product), allocation share 1,
   allocated investment 10,000, projected recovery 1,200 (2 x lifetime demand 600) and under-recovery
   -8,800. The separate view of the same settings returns the same project investment 10,000, a
   separate charge of 10,000 and no amortized contribution, projected recovery or under/over
   recovery: the investment is never counted twice or recovered through both channels at once. The
   tooling settings are unchanged after both views.
6. **A16 tooling productivity.** With entered base price 100 and the SB-17 amortized contribution 2,
   the first-year total unit price is 102. In the exempt scenario year 2 is 97 + 2 = 99; in the
   scenario whose productivity applies to both the base price and the contribution year 2 is
   97 + 2 x 0.97 = 98.94. The comparison also shows the contribution series 2, 2, 2 versus 2, 1.94,
   1.8818.
7. **A17 price visibility.** In the amortized-exempt scenario the product line shows the suggested
   first-year price 100 beside the manual first-year price 100 and the manual EBIT amount 20 / rate
   0.2. In the separate-charge scenario, manual price 125, suggested price 100, manual EBIT amount 45
   and rate 0.36 are all present. The blank-manual-price scenario returns manual price `null`, manual
   EBIT amount `null`, manual EBIT rate `null`, and `null` quoted revenue/EBIT for every year, not
   zero; the suggested first-year price 100 stays visible and the scenario result remains `complete`.
8. **A19 scenario integrity.** The comparison names its common immutable baseline and reports the
   shared project volume policy `average`, EBIT target 0.2 and productivity rate 0.03. All five named
   scenarios are evaluated against that one baseline and are directly comparable (`stale` false). A
   later product forecast edit auto-publishes a newer project version and draft snapshot; the
   dependency report then flags every saved scenario with `scenario_baseline_outdated` and
   `scenario_project_outdated`, and the comparison against the original baseline still evaluates the
   scenarios but marks each `stale` with those reasons. Comparing the same scenarios against the
   newer draft labels them `scenario_baseline_differs` and `scenario_project_version_differs` instead
   of presenting the results as equivalent.
9. **R-DEM-01/02/03.** The project owns the volume policy and the product schedule overrides: DEM's
   project default schedule is 2027-2027 while both products override it to 2027-2028; MISSING's
   project default 2027-2028 is inherited by its product (schedule_override null). Demand is summed by
   calendar year across products before the policy (A11), missing years are incomplete and no zero
   rows are introduced (A12). Annual demand 300/300, representative costing volume 300 and lifetime
   demand 600 are three distinct returned values.
10. **R-MFG-04.** Setup is the dedicated setup duration x setup rate as a separate charge, counted
    once per shared part routing per quotation; the two producing products share the routing and the
    setup total stays 60 (A13).
11. **R-TOOL-01/02/03/04/05.** Investment is tool count x cost per tool (all six ADVANCED entries sum
    to 14,000 once; the COM tool is 10,000 once). Recovery supports the combined lifetime-demand
    basis (COM 600; ADVANCED product-root tool 100; repeated-occurrence tool 50 x 2 = 100) and a
    manual positive quantity (5,000 -> 2; 100 -> 100). A shared tool is allocated by lifetime demand
    shares (W-1/A: combined 150, shares 2/3 and 1/3, allocated 666.666...667 + 333.333...333 = 1,000
    for a 1,000 investment) without multiplying the investment or changing the recovery quantity.
    Both the separate-charge and amortized alternatives are available through the same read-only
    evaluation and the same saved settings; a tool whose owner sits only inside a purchased branch
    reports `zero_combined_demand` with an unavailable recovery quantity. The quoted contribution
    continues through the production period after the recovery quantity is reached: recovery
    quantity 100 gives 100 per unit and annual recovery revenue 10,000 / 20,000 / 30,000, projected
    recovery 60,000 and over-recovery 50,000.
12. **R-COST-01/02/03.** At representative costing volume 300 the purchase-tier source with the
    greatest minimum not exceeding the volume (minimum 250) is selected; the 8 USD tier converts
    through the saved 7.2 rate to 57.6 project currency, while the same-currency catalog price 80 has
    `fx_rate` null and project price 80 (identity). A published newer production rate version (200
    CNY/h) is not adopted automatically: a later DEM recalculation still pins the manufacturing
    record's rate version (the seeded 120 CNY/h version) and the earlier baseline snapshot is
    unchanged. Uncovered demand with no covering tier and no single price is `missing_purchase_price`
    with an unavailable unit cost, never zero.
13. **R-COST-07/08.** In ADVANCED the purchased assembly PA-1/A (price 50) covers its children: the
    direct child C-1/A contributes demand only from product Y (50, never the 150 that would include
    X's purchased branch), the nested-only part D-1/A reports no demand and no cost issue, and the
    same C-1/A part used directly by Y still contributes. The category configuration is evaluated
    once on the finished unit: the direct-material view is a view and is not added again (X unit
    total 62.2 = 52 material + 5 fixed + 5.2 percentage, not 114.2), Y is 29.2 = 22 + 5 + 2.2, and the
    10% category uses the material+production subtotal. A circular percentage basis and a duplicated
    category code are refused with 422 and nothing is written.
14. Two deliberate contract refusals were observed and are expected, not defects: a zero manual base
    price is 422 (`a manual base price must be strictly positive; leave it out for a blank price`)
    and an EBIT target of 100% is 422 (`the EBIT target must stay below 100%`).
