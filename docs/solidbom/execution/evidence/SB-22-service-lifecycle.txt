## Limitations and defects

- No defect was found in the routes exercised by this run; every acceptance check passed.
- Recorded observation, not a defect: the accepted-baseline export labels the saved scenario
  `stale_scenarios` because the scenario is pinned to the accepted baseline while a newer draft
  baseline exists. The exported result itself is not stale; SB-18's comparability rule is applied
  and the scenario identity is still printed.
- Recorded observation, not a defect: the coordinated activation's pinned project-wide costing
  snapshot is `incomplete` because another quotation-included product (D) had no active revision
  at that moment; the activation contract deliberately exposes the project-wide state rather than
  hiding it, and the D activation's snapshot is `complete`.
- The desktop interactions (disconnect/retry, desktop export command, UI labels) are outside this
  service run and are recorded as Unrun above where they are required; their prerequisite is the
  SB-22 desktop acceptance run (task-4).
