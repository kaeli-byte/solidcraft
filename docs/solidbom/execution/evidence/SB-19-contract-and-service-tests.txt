The twelve activation domain tests cover:

- a shared sourcing/material change used by two active products refuses an A-only activation with

- the three-product bypass regression: A and B share the changed part and C is independent, so a

  activation succeeds and A can activate alone afterwards;
- a fully priced, feasible part with an unconfirmed classification is blocked until the engineer
  confirms it;
- a missing cost input (the shared part's allowance) blocks every member of the group and writes
  nothing until fixed;
- an unresolved feasibility finding blocks every member until a recorded justified exception
  resolves the disposition, and no negotiated selling price or scenario is needed;
- distinct customer revisions stay distinct part versions, the pending change on one revision's part
  requires the product that uses it even when the selected product does not, an invented revision is
  an `invalid_request` refusal and a foreign revision is a `revision_not_activatable` refusal, all
  without writing;
- only the newest draft revision appended to a product is activatable; a second delivery makes the
  older draft superseded and both readiness and activation refuse it on both routes without writing;
- stale project version, stale accepted baseline, request-id payload reuse and an injected failure
  after the project-version insert all write nothing (the injected failure plants a competing
  accepted row so the final immutable insert violates a unique constraint inside the transaction);
- the accepted history is newest-first, a historical baseline is unchanged after later edits, and an
  unknown baseline is not found;
- the readiness coordination report names the changed definition aspects and the required products.

## Fixes applied for the independent verification findings

- **B1 (blocker) pending-change coordination bypass:** changed candidates are the accepted-baseline
  head's whole `shared_definitions` pin set, compared through the contract's
  `pending_shared_definition_changes` helper, so any activation while a change is pending must
  include every quotation-included product whose resulting costed structure uses a changed part, even
  when the request's own members do not. The three-product regression is in `tests/activation.rs`.
- **S2 current draft:** only the newest (last) draft revision appended to a product is activatable; an
  older superseded draft is `revision_not_activatable` on both routes.
- **S3 flaky test:** the two-product assertions find members by `product_id`; the activation suite was
  re-run repeatedly and the full service suite run twice on the rebased tree.
- **S4 contract payload sentence:** the contract now states the shared axum `Json<T>` extractor's
  plain `400` before any handler for a non-deserializable body.
- **N1 receipt facts:** exactly one `baseline_accepted` first, revision facts in member order, then
  `shared_definition_changed` sorted by part version id; the amended validation enforces it.
- **N3 single resolution:** activation applies the member actions in memory, then resolves the SB-16
  baseline once from the resulting project version and reuses it for the coordination check, the
  readiness recheck, the accepted pins and the pinned snapshot.
- **N4 repository gates:** `SB-19-repository-gates.txt` records the gates and the honest full-`ci`
  limitation.
- **Evidence corrections (round 2):** the native evidence adds an isolated fully-priced/feasible
  project whose only blocker is the unconfirmed classification, the mixed blocked report is labelled
  honestly, and the repository-gate scope statements were corrected.
