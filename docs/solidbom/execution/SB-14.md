increment satisfies them as follows:

- **Base and owner:** branch `agent/feasibility-rules-5230ca6b5a04`, starting commit `7fa5a0ab2`;
  the worktree and shared edits are reserved in this record. No other package's record, generated
  file or ledger row is edited.
- **Locked contract:** `docs/solidbom/contracts/v2/feasibility-rules-and-exceptions.md` at
  `3477efc44` (contract version 1, additive to API version 2), registered in the contract registry
  with its portable types, producers, consumers and acceptance.
- **Rules:** Administrator-published append-only rule-table versions with structured applicability
  and typed limits; every rule keeps its required source reference. No priority, override or
  interpolation exists: inapplicable rules never pass, and overlapping limits conflict until an
  Administrator publishes a narrower version.
- **Applicability evidence:** tube kind, material name, supporting spring and nominal sizes are
  optional reviewed evidence; a rule whose conditions are not proven does not apply. The supplied
  smooth-tube table is only usable with standard PA12 and a supporting spring, and the corrugated
  table's automated measurement is outside the SB-12 scope. The extractor never rounds a measured
  size into a nominal one.
- **Measurements:** every bend radius is its own finding, spacing is the tangent-to-tangent SB-12
  value, both end straight lengths and both connector checks are evaluated, and missing
  measurements, connector associations or limits are unresolved. `FeasibilityTubeMeasurements`
  refuses zero-length substitutes and inconsistent counts.
- **Exceptions:** an Engineer may accept a justified exception for a failing, conflicting or
  missing-limit finding; a pass, a missing measurement and a missing connector association cannot be
  excepted. The authenticated identity is the reviewer (never a payload field). The source's
  proposal about reopening exceptions is locked explicitly: the accepted evidence is compared with
  the current reviewed binding and the current assessment on every read, and a change produces
  visible `review_required` with the reason, never a silent keep or drop. There is no automatic
  re-acceptance, time expiry or extra approval workflow.
- **Readiness:** issues are computed on read for SB-19 (`feasibility_not_assessed`,
  `assessment_stale`, `open_finding_failed`, `open_finding_conflict`, `open_finding_unresolved`,
  `exception_review_required`); the service publishes no activation decision.
- **Independence from costing:** no value in the contract names a cost center or a rate.
