# SB-16 — Shared demand and manufacturing costs

Status: planned; not ready for assignment.

## Objective

Calculate an explainable manufacturing baseline from shared calendar-year demand, explicit sourcing and versioned inputs.

## Requirements

Read [APP_SPECIFICATION](../requirements/cost_calculator_handover/APP_SPECIFICATION.md), sections 10–12 and 14; O04/O09/O10/O14; scenarios 4 and 11–13. Workbook totals are references only where compatible.

## Dependencies

Depends on: SB-15

Start with integrated sourcing/routing, project and reference-version contracts.

## Scope

Aggregate quoted-product component demand by calendar year before applying project average/maximum/median policy. Require positive forecasts in inclusive product SOP–EOP, omitting years outside applicable schedules. Calculate purchase tiers, saved manual FX, geometric/manual net consumption plus allowance, operation costs and configured categories. Stop descendant demand/cost/tooling expansion within purchased branches. Count each shared part routing's setup once per project quotation, with assembly setups at their own level. Preserve immutable results and incomplete states.

## Interfaces

Lock annual-demand maps keyed by part/customer version, resolved cost-policy inputs and explained part/product/project outputs. Expose deterministic backend calculation plus impacted-draft recalculation entry points for SB-20. Retain selected version IDs, FX direction, quantity/units and missing issues in snapshots. Tooling recovery and commercial prices remain separate consumers.

## Ownership

Own proposed pure demand/manufacturing costing modules, server calculation adapters and existing area tests. Reserve shared calculator contracts, category configuration, migrations and result UI registrations. Do not alter SB-15 routing semantics independently. Own `execution/SB-16.md` only.

## Acceptance

- A demands 100/200 and B 200/100: shared annual demand is 300/300 and peak 300, not 400. Missing forecasts do not become zero; schedules do not create zero padding.
- FX multiplies source amounts by project-currency units per source unit; allowance increases material consumption once. A purchase tier is the greatest covered minimum quantity; absent coverage stays missing.
- Purchased parents retain CAD children but suppress that branch's downstream costs/demand; the same child elsewhere still counts. Setup 0.5 h ×120 is 60 once, separate from unit cost. Test nested rollups, repeatability, incomplete results and native cost explanations.

## Assignment gate

Require integrated SB-15, locked aggregate/category/FX/rounding and result contracts, reserved files and independent numeric fixtures. No automatic rate inflation, volume-driven rate switching, CAPEX or legacy tooling amortization may enter this baseline.

## Handoff

Record arithmetic and demand contracts, input versions, reconciliation fixtures, actual root/service checks and evidence. Supply SB-17/SB-19/SB-20 with immutable baseline and incompleteness interfaces.
