# SB-11 — Engineering review and flat grouping

Status: planned; not ready for assignment.

## Objective

Support engineer-reviewed component associations and measurements for both preserved assemblies and flattened STEP data.

## Requirements

Read [APP_SPECIFICATION](../requirements/cost_calculator_handover/APP_SPECIFICATION.md), sections 6–8 and acceptance scenarios 4, 6 and 8. Collection of confirmed labels does not authorize model training/deployment.

## Dependencies

Depends on: SB-09, SB-10

Both predecessors must be integrated with their reference, authorization and identity contracts.

## Scope

Add a flat component review for inputs without usable hierarchy, with engineer-confirmed solid/surface associations stored separately from original CAD. Preserve valid customer trees. Extend shared engineering records with manual measurement completion, classification correction, bulk confirmation and confirmed-label capture. Keep individual occurrences and shared part versions distinct. Recognition algorithms belong to SB-13; geometry extraction belongs to SB-12.

## Interfaces

Lock reviewed association, measurement-source/unit/review and confirmed-label records against source/analysis and part-version identities. Expose typed group/confirm/update actions and bidirectional tree/viewport selection adapters. Define persisted training-example metadata as a technical contract without claiming automatic training or retroactive reclassification.

## Ownership

Own proposed engineering-review/grouping server and client modules, selection adapters specific to reviewed groups and existing area tests. SB-12 owns extraction algorithms. Coordinate manifest/schema extensions, shared selection wiring and migrations through reservations. Own `execution/SB-11.md` only.

## Acceptance

- Several solids may be confirmed as one component; unresolved associations remain uncosted and no nested customer hierarchy is invented.
- Repeated occurrences share reviewed metadata while preserving their placements; selecting associated geometry resolves the reviewed record.
- Open-surface volume stays missing until manually completed and reviewed. Bulk confirmation records engineer decisions; high confidence cannot confirm automatically. Verify save/reopen and source hash preservation with native evidence.

## Assignment gate

Require integrated SB-09/SB-10, locked grouping/selection/provenance contracts, reserved shared files and an assigned owner/base. Explicitly choose validation for overlapping or incomplete associations before assignment; do not infer one body equals one component.

## Handoff

Record confirmed-association invariants, persisted label/measurement examples, commands, executed checks and UI evidence. Identify remaining uncertain groups and contracts consumed by SB-13/SB-14/SB-15.
