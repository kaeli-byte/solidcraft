# SB-17 — Tooling investment and recovery

Status: planned; not ready for assignment.

## Objective

Track each tooling investment once and explain its allocation and separate or amortized recovery.

## Requirements

Read [APP_SPECIFICATION](../requirements/cost_calculator_handover/APP_SPECIFICATION.md), section 12, O02/O03 and scenarios 15–16. Confirmed recovery rules supersede workbook Year1 × year-span amortization.

## Dependencies

Depends on: SB-16

Use integrated expanded quantities, lifetime demand and manufacturing baseline contracts.

## Scope

Assign each tool entry to one part definition or assembly, with unique identity and count-times-unit investment. Allocate shared investment by each quoted product's lifetime component-demand share. Support separate charge or unit contribution, lifetime/manual positive recovery quantity, assembly ownership and projected under/over-recovery. Continue contribution over the production period without automatic cap/stop. Multi-part/family tools, production-sales tracking and project machine CAPEX are excluded.

## Interfaces

Lock tool ownership/investment, allocation and scenario recovery-input/result contracts, respecting purchased-branch exclusions. Provide deterministic investment/allocation/recovery functions consumed by SB-18; keep investment, recurring cost and recovery revenue distinct. The specification labels the detailed contribution formula as a proposal: lock that calculation contract explicitly before assignment while preserving confirmed examples and no-double-recovery behavior.

## Ownership

Own proposed tooling domain/calculator modules, tooling editing/explanation UI and area tests. Reserve shared snapshot types, migrations and scenario interface changes with SB-18's integration owner. Own `execution/SB-17.md` only.

## Acceptance

- Two tools at 10,000 produce 20,000 investment once; repeated/shared occurrences cannot multiply the project investment.
- A 10,000 investment with recovery quantity 5,000 contributes 2 per component unit under the locked formula. Product allocation shares use lifetime demand; missing/zero combined demand is incomplete.
- Switching separate/amortized treatment preserves investment and does not recover it twice. Forecasts show under/over-recovery without automatic stopping; assembly quantities and purchased cutoffs are respected. Capture native investment/recovery evidence.

## Assignment gate

Require integrated SB-16, locked allocation/recovery/precision contracts, reserved shared files and an assigned base commit. Do not silently resolve a proposed formula or introduce multi-part ownership.

## Handoff

Record tool identities, formula version, scenario input boundaries, independent numeric checks and evidence. Give SB-18 recovery outputs and SB-19 completeness information without requiring negotiated selling prices.
