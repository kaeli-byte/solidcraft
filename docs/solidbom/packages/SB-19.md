# SB-19 — Activation and accepted baselines

Status: planned; not ready for assignment.

## Objective

Activate complete engineering/costing revisions atomically while preserving coherent shared definitions and prior accepted results.

## Requirements

Read [APP_SPECIFICATION](../requirements/cost_calculator_handover/APP_SPECIFICATION.md), section 6, O05/O06 and scenarios 1, 3 and 5. Atomic transaction mechanics are a technical choice implementing confirmed coordinated activation.

## Dependencies

Depends on: SB-14, SB-16, SB-17

Consume integrated feasibility disposition, complete costing and tooling inputs plus their project identities.

## Scope

Determine readiness from required cost inputs, engineer-confirmed classifications and resolved or justified findings. Consume SB-11 review status; a feasible, fully priced but unconfirmed classification is not ready. Permit the preparing Engineer to activate without a separate approval role. Coordinate every affected active product for shared sourcing/material/dimension/routing changes within one customer part version; unrelated product-only changes may activate independently. Retain earlier active/history until successful activation. Negotiated price and completed commercial scenarios are not prerequisites.

## Interfaces

Lock readiness reports, affected-product sets and an expected-version activation request/receipt. Activation commits a consistent group of accepted references/results or none. Define concurrency checks and historic baseline reads; expose changed-baseline facts to SB-20. Separate customer revisions remain separately shared groups.

## Ownership

Own proposed activation/readiness service, activation review UI and area tests. Reserve transaction/schema migrations, shared baseline contracts and reference update operations. SB-14 owns findings; SB-16/SB-17 own cost completeness. Own `execution/SB-19.md` only.

## Acceptance

- Shared sourcing changes affect A/B: activating A alone is refused; complete coordinated activation changes both or neither.
- Pending classifications, missing cost inputs or unresolved findings block every member. Test a fully priced, feasible part with an unconfirmed classification. Recorded justified exceptions can resolve disposition; missing negotiated price does not block activation.
- A conflict/failure mid-activation preserves previous active references and history. Different genuine customer revisions remain independent; inventing a revision cannot bypass coordination. Capture native readiness/activation evidence.

## Assignment gate

Require integrated predecessors, locked readiness/grouping/transaction contracts, reserved shared edits and an assigned base commit. Do not adopt unapproved transfer/provenance mechanics without recording their technical resolution.

## Handoff

Record readiness semantics, transactional invariants, conflict tests, actual integrated results and evidence. Supply SB-20 baseline-change and historical-read contracts; do not label unintegrated branches verified.
